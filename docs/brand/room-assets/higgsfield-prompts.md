# Room furniture: Higgsfield prompts

Art for the pet's room (Home) and the room shop (v3 design, turns 13 and 15).

## Shared style (prepend to every prompt)

> Single piece of pet furniture, soft 3D clay-toy render, rounded chunky shapes, matte finish, gentle studio lighting from upper left, soft contact shadow underneath. Seen straight from the front, camera slightly above, as if looking into a small room (one-point perspective). Brand colours: warm orange #FF7A1A to #F2600C, white, soft light grey; small accents allowed. Isolated on a plain transparent background, centred, no text, no logos, no floor, no room. Square 1024×1024.

Generate every piece in the same session and with the same seed family, so they all match.
