# Opaw privacy policy

## What stays on your phone

Your dog's profile, photos, care logs, walks, routes, reminders and memories are stored only on your
phone. The photo cutout for the avatar is made on the device (with Apple's Vision on iPhone, and
Google's on-device ML Kit on Android).

## Location

Opaw uses your location only while you record a walk or look at the Pack map. On Android, a walk keeps
recording with the screen off through an ongoing notification you can see and stop at any time. Routes
stay on your phone unless you share a walk with the Pack.

## What leaves your phone, and only if you choose

- **The Pack (optional).** When you join the Pack, Opaw creates an anonymous account (no email or phone
  number). Anything you share is stored on Opaw's server (Supabase): your display name, your dog's
  name, breed, size and photo, and an approximate location rounded to about 1 km. Walks you share
  include distance, time and a simplified route with the start and end removed. Memories you share
  include the photo and caption. Likes, follows, blocks and reports are stored so the Pack works and
  stays safe.
- **Motion clips (optional).** If you generate motion clips, your dog's cutout is uploaded so the
  avatar worker can create the clips. The clips are stored privately for your account.
- **Purchases.** Opaw Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Opaw never sees your
  payment details.
- **Maps.** On Android, map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, which sees the map area being loaded, not who
  you are.

Opaw does not track you across other apps or websites, does not show ads and does not sell data.

## Deleting your data

In the Pack, open the settings menu and choose **Leave the pack and delete my account**. This
deletes your account, shared dogs, posts, likes, follows and uploaded photos from the server.
**Settings > Reset data** erases everything stored on your phone, and so does deleting the app.
