# Opaw privacy policy

## What stays on your iPhone

Your dog's profile, photos, care logs, walks, routes, reminders and memories are stored only on your
iPhone. The photo cutout for the avatar is made on the device.

## What leaves your iPhone, and only if you choose

- **The Pack (optional).** When you join the Pack, Opaw creates an anonymous account (no email or phone
  number). Anything you share is stored on Opaw's server (Supabase): your display name, your dog's
  name, breed, size and photo, and an approximate location rounded to about 1 km. Walks you share
  include distance, time and a simplified route with the start and end removed. Memories you share
  include the photo and caption. Likes, follows, blocks and reports are stored so the Pack works and
  stays safe.
- **Motion clips (optional).** If you generate motion clips, your dog's cutout is uploaded so the
  avatar worker can create the clips. The clips are stored privately for your account.

Opaw does not track you across other apps or websites, does not show ads and does not sell data.

## Deleting your data

In the Pack tab, open the settings menu and choose **Leave the pack and delete my account**. This
deletes your account, shared dogs, posts, likes, follows and uploaded photos from the server. Deleting
the app deletes everything stored on your iPhone.
